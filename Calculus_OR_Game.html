Professor Diabolus Von Failsworth III is back. After his defeat in the ER, he slipped The Accumulator into Ms. Cindy's coffee. It's a toxin that makes every vital sign pile up over time.

Your surgical team has six procedures to get through. First you'll estimate her blood loss with Riemann sums. Then you'll use antiderivatives and the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus to dose her medicine, fight the infection, restart her heart, and bring her oxygen back.

Every correct answer raises Ms. Cindy's Stability. Reach 70% by the end and she walks out of the hospital. Fall short and Von Failsworth takes over your class. Forever.
